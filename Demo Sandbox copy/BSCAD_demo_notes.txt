ffmpeg incantation to make MacOS screenshots acceptable to Gitter.  Rename 
"Screen Reording <some date-time-group>.mov"  to "demo.mov", then:

ffmpeg -i "demo.mov" \
  -vf "scale=1280:-2,fps=30"\
  -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 26 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -c:a aac -b:a 128k \
  -movflags +faststart \
  out.mp4
